If you only land two things

The moments that sell it

The intake gate Beat 3

It solves a political problem, not a technical one. Everyone in that room has argued about why a project hasn't started. Thirty seconds after he sees seven named fields with seven named owners, that argument is over.

The blocked lane Beat 4

It is the entire ZD Universe strategy in one click. Build the whole pipe, light one segment at a time — and the segments still dark are busy generating the business case for lighting them.

Be first to say it

What isn't built

Don't let him find these. Name them before he does — it buys credibility for everything you did build.

Eleven of the Manual's eighteen sections are live. Not built: §8 Value Engineering · §10 BOQ endorsement · §12 finishing items & sampling · §16 QA/QC · §17 regulatory visits · §18 closeout and as-built. No notifications, no search, no file upload, no LLR library.

Also say plainly: task progress is demo data. The catalog, the spine, the routes, the thresholds, the people and the conflicts are all real. The dates are not.

Twenty questions

What you need from him

Group A — blocking

Nothing SLA-shaped can be defended until these two are settled. Get them in the room, in writing, today.

01

Which document is canonical on TAT — the Booklet or the Manual? Blocking

They disagree on 6 of 10 architecture stages, and the Manual contradicts its own flowcharts on stages 5, 11 and 12. One switch in the code applies his ruling.

02

Is a "day" a working day or a calendar day — and does TAT include waiting on outsiders? Blocking

No document states it. Built assuming working days, Mon–Fri, excluding external wait. If he says otherwise it's one function.

Group B — scope

Each of these changes what the system covers, and two of them are schema decisions rather than preferences.

03

Is Creative in or out?

Seven of Creative's twelve workflows have no project to attach to. Currently parked on a "Department BAU" carrier — that's a placeholder, not an answer.

04

Does the spine cover acquisition and construction support, or just stages 3–12?

His Manual's matrix stops at 12, but §3 and §§5–18 are clearly his department, and construction support runs for years.

05

Is there a Volume 2 of the Workflow Booklet?

Vol. 1 is labelled as such. Ahmed's session was described as incomplete.

06

Who writes the §4.2 Internal Checklist Annexure, and by when?

It's mandatory at every stage and required before any drawing issuance — and it says "(to be added)". The engine is built and ships empty; his team authors the checks in-system.

Group C — how it should behave

Confirm the calls already made. If he disagrees, better now than after forty people are using it.

07

Hard gates or soft gates?

Built soft — every refusal offers "Override & proceed" with a mandatory reason that lands in the ledger. A hard block just pushes work back to paper.

08

Files — stored in the system, or a register that links to the drive?

Built as register + link. Becoming a document management system is a different product.

09

Who outside the 44 needs access — contractors, consultants, the Authority?

Portal accounts, or internal-only with their side logged manually?

10

Do site visits need to work on a phone?

§15 mandates monthly visits with photos. Currently desk entry.

Group D — people and data

Cheap to answer, and each one unblocks something concrete.

11

Name the approval authorities.

Head ZD, Director Design, Director Projects, Audit head, SCM head, CPC members. Real names to real accounts.

12

What are the other four projects?

The booklet says twelve on-ground; the cover names eight. The rest weren't invented.

13

Should we start from Zergham's partial ZD build, or fork clean?

Khurram's email said he'd share it. Still unseen.

Group E — the universe

These reach past his department. If the right people aren't in the room, find out who is and get the meeting.

14

SAGE — can we get API or database access? Program-shaping

The single biggest unknown. Stores and Procurement are built on it. If the answer is no, phases 5–6 become manual double-entry.

15

Primavera — import milestones, or schedule inside the system?

16

Does anything called "ZD Universe" already exist?

PM Manual §8.2.1 mandates it as if it does — "the primary central hub… single source of truth". Partial build, a Drive folder, or purely aspirational?

17

Who arbitrates when two manuals contradict each other?

MAR means three different things across three manuals. CFT means two. There are two separate LLR processes. There will be more.

18

Should the CPC queue move into the system?

Thursdays, every department, everything over PKR 500,000. Probably the single biggest quick win available across the whole universe.

19

Does Audit get read access across all four departments?

They appear as a control in every one of the four manuals.

20

One platform with four departments on it — or four separate systems? Decides everything

The shared case engine, the two clocks, value routing, the CPC queue and the cross-department ledger all evaporate if MAR gets built three times in three codebases. His own PM Manual §8.2.1 is the argument for one.
